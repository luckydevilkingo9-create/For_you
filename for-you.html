A Letter

Sanjeevani, your name means the giver of life — and somehow you've spent every day since we met proving it true. You breathe color into things I used to walk right past without noticing.

I don't know how to explain it simply, so I won't try. It's in the way my day rearranges itself around the thought of you. It's in how a place stops being just a place the moment you're in it. It's in the quiet, unglamorous minutes — you, half-listening to me ramble — that somehow became my favorite minutes in this entire life.

People spend their whole lives looking for something steady to hold on to. I found mine in you, and I am not letting go.

So today isn't really about flowers or a screen full of animations. It's just me, trying, in my own clumsy way, to say: thank you for choosing to love me back.

Yours, always
I love you.
Today, and every day after this one.
